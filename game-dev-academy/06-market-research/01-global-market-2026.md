# The Global Games Market in 2026

> **What you'll learn**
> - How big the games market is (about **$214B in 2026**), split by mobile, console and PC, and where it is growing.
> - What actually happened in 2025–2026: layoffs, live-service burnout, pricier hardware, GTA VI, and UGC platforms eating playtime.
> - What all of that means for a solo Roblox developer making games for kids.

**Tags:** **[P]** the company said it · **[S]** analyst or reputable press · **[U]** unverified. All figures as of Oct 2026 unless noted. Re-check anything older than 6 months.

## 1. The headline numbers

**The short version:** the market is big, growing slowly (about 6% a year), and mobile is more than half of it. Growth now comes from getting **existing players to spend more**, not from finding new players.

### Market size by segment (Newzoo, consumer spending, USD)

| Segment | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 forecast | Growth 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mobile** | ~$100B (derived) | $103B → revised up | **$121.1B** | +6.8% |
| **Console** | ~$43.5B (derived) | $45.9B → revised | **$46.9B** | +5.1% |
| **PC** | ~$38.9B (derived) | $39.9B → revised up (PC grew ~12%) | **$45.9B** | +5.3% |
| **Total** | ~$182.6B (derived) | **$201.6B** (final estimate) | **$213.9B** | +6.1% |

Sources and notes:
- 2026 forecast, 2025 final estimate of $201.6B, and "PC grew 12% in 2025": Newzoo Global Games Market Report 2026, summarized Aug 25, 2026 [S] ([GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-the-gaming-market-in-2026), [Wccftech interview](https://wccftech.com/newzoo-2026-global-games-market-report-gta-6-interview/)).
- Newzoo's September 2025 edition had 2025 at $188.8B (+3.4%); mobile $103B, console $45.9B, PC $39.9B [S] ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/newzoo-global-games-market-set-to-hit-1888bn-in-2025/)). It later revised 2025 up to $197B, then to $201.6B.
- **2024 column is derived by us** by dividing the Sept 2025 figures by their stated growth rates. Newzoo restates past years, so treat 2024 as a rough guide only.

> ⚠️ **Forecasts move.** Newzoo's 2025 number went from $188.8B to $201.6B in a year. Quote the year *and* the edition.

### A counterpoint on mobile

Newzoo says mobile grows ~7% in 2026. App-store trackers see something flatter:

- **Sensor Tower, full-year 2025:** mobile game in-app spending up about 1% to ~$82B. Non-game apps ($85.6B) passed games in in-app revenue for the first time [S] ([GameDev Reports summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)).
- **Sensor Tower, H1 2026:** mobile game IAP about $39.8B vs $40.6B a year earlier (≈ −2%), downloads down ~12% [S] ([Naavik](https://naavik.substack.com/p/h1-2026-mobile-gamings-stability), [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-the-gaming-market-in)).
- **Why they disagree:** Sensor Tower counts App Store + Google Play only. Newzoo also counts web shops (direct-to-consumer), third-party Android stores and China Android [S] (same GameDev Reports Newzoo summary).

**Takeaway:** mobile app-store spending is roughly flat; growth is happening off-store and on platforms like Roblox.

## 4. The state of the industry, 2025–2026

### Layoffs are still high

- About **9,200** games jobs cut in 2025, down from ~15,600 in 2024 [U] ([Wikipedia tracker table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932026_video_game_industry_layoffs)).
- 2026 is worse again: ~10,600 cut by Oct 10, 2026, with a full-year projection near 13,800 (Amir Satvat's tracker; projections have swung between ~8,000 and ~14,700 this year) [U] ([GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/4731-new-data-suggests-layoffs-will-affect-14259-people-by-the-end-of-2026-up-78/), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27176/five-years-post-pandemic-57000-games-industry-jobs-have-vanished)).
- GDC 2026 survey: **28%** of developers were laid off in the past two years (33% in the US). **52%** say generative AI is hurting the industry, up from 30% a year earlier [S] ([GDC](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/), [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260129438528/en/2026-State-of-the-Game-Industry-Report-Reveals-Widening-Effect-of-Layoffs-Broader-Perspectives-on-Generative-AI-Unionization-Tariffs-and-More)).

### Old games keep the players ("live-service saturation")

- On PC and console, **new releases get only ~13% of playtime**, and that share has been flat for three years. The top 20 franchises still take more than half [S] (Newzoo PC & Console Report 2026, [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-pc-and-console-market-in-2026)).
- In 2025 **Roblox became the most-played title on PC/console by hours (+52% YoY)**, passing Fortnite (−29%) and Call of Duty (−33%) [S] (same).
- Battle-royale playtime fell 27%; sandbox playtime rose 36% [S] (same).
- Many 2025–2026 live-service launches failed or shut down quickly (e.g. Anthem servers closed Jan 2026) [U] ([GamesFuze tally](https://gamesfuze.com/game-news/how-many-live-service-games-have-actually-shut-down-in-2026/)). Counts vary by source.

**Why it matters:** players don't have free hours for another "forever game". A new game must either *steal* time from a habit, or fit into short gaps.

### UGC platforms are rising

- Roblox 2025: bookings **$6.8B (+55%)**, revenue $4.9B, and **$1.5B paid to creators** via DevEx [P] ([Roblox Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)).
- Fortnite: player-made islands took **47% of all Fortnite hours in May 2026** (38% a year earlier). Epic has paid **$1B+** to UEFN creators since 2023 [S] ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/unreal-engine-for-fortnite-creator-payouts-surpass-1bn/)).
- But not all UGC worlds survive: **Rec Room shut down on June 1, 2026** [S] ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/social-gaming-platform-rec-room-once-valued-at-3-5b-is-shutting-down/)).
- Details in `03-ugc-platforms-compared.md`.

### Roblox's 2026 wobble (important for Sam)

After a record 2025, Roblox hit a slowdown in 2026:

| Quarter | DAUs | Bookings | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q3 2025 | 151.5M (record) | — | Grow a Garden / Steal a Brainrot peak [S] ([TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/roblox-sinks-59-in-a-year-as-q3-guidance-points-to-an-18-bookings-drop)) |
| Q4 2025 | 144M (+69%) | $2.22B (+63%) | [P] Q4 letter |
| Q1 2026 | 132M (+35%) | $1.73B (+43%) | [P] ([Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm)) |
| Q2 2026 | 123M (+10%) | $1.56B (+8%) | [P] ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)) |
| Q3 2026 guide | "sequential increase" | **$1.58–1.65B (−14% to −18%)** | [P] Q2 letter. Results due Oct 29, 2026 [S] |

Roblox's own reasons [P] (Q2 2026 letter):
- Mandatory **age checks** for chat (from Jan 2026) slowed engagement.
- Spending **per hour fell, most among younger kids in the US and Canada**.
- Players moved from 2025's viral hits to new and evergreen games that monetize less per hour.
- The **Recommended For You** algorithm now judges games over **28 days (was 7)** and adds **play-through rate and first-play bounce rate**. It favours games that keep players over games that squeeze them.

**For Sam:** Roblox is now paying discovery to *retention*, not to short-term spend. That suits honest, kid-friendly design.

### Hardware got more expensive

- Nintendo raised the US Switch 2 price from $449.99 to **$499.99 on Sept 1, 2026**, blaming memory costs and tariffs, and forecast lower Switch 2 sales (16.5M units for the fiscal year) [S] ([Engadget](https://engadget.com/2167631/nintendo-raises-switch-2-prices), [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/nintendo-is-raising-switch-2-prices-around-the-world)).
- PS5 and Xbox Series prices also rose in 2026 (reported PS5 disc $649.99, Xbox Series X $799.99) [U] ([Gizmogo](https://www.gizmogo.com/blog/gaming-console-prices-2026-sell-to-offset-higher-costs/), [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/console-price-increase-xbox-switch-2-2026/)). Check official stores before quoting.
- US console hardware spending fell 29% in July 2026 to $282M (Circana) [U] (reported via [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/console-sales-13-year-low-ram-prices-2026/)).
- AAA software: most standard PS5/Xbox games still $69.99; $79.99 is mostly premium editions and some Switch 2 titles [U] (same secondary sources).

**For Sam:** pricier consoles push more kids toward the free device they already have: **a phone or tablet**. Mobile-first is the right bet.

### GTA VI is (still) coming

- Take-Two's latest filings list **Grand Theft Auto VI for November 19, 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. PC date not announced [P] ([Take-Two 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026054870/ttwo-20260630.htm)).
- Newzoo expects it to lift console full-game spending ~17.5% in 2026; if it slips, console likely shrinks [S] ([GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-the-gaming-market-in-2026)).
- GTA VI is rated for adults, so it is *not* direct competition for Sam's under-13 players. Their older siblings and parents will be busy, though, and YouTube will be full of it in late Nov–Dec 2026.

## 5. Where the money is moving

| Moving **toward** | Moving **away from** |
|---|---|
| UGC platforms (Roblox, Fortnite islands) | Big new live-service launches |
| Spending more per existing player | Paying to acquire new players |
| Web shops / D2C on mobile | Pure app-store mobile |
| Cheap PC games: sub-$30 PC segment +40% (2022–25) [S] ([Newzoo via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-pc-and-console-market-in-2026)) | Mid-budget "AA" games with no hook |
| Subscriptions (US +20% in 2025) [S] | Physical discs (30-year low) |
| Emerging regions (MEA, LatAm, India, Japan on Roblox) | Saturated US/EU new-user growth |

On Roblox specifically: Q2 2026 DAUs grew **67% in Japan** and **64% in India**, but only **6% in US/Canada** [P] (Q2 2026 letter). Note that Roblox also says players outside US/Canada monetize less per hour.

## 6. What this means for a solo / indie dev (Sam)

1. **Go where the players already are.** New games get ~13% of PC/console hours. On Roblox, discovery is built in and paid out of a $1.5B/year creator pool. Don't start a standalone game unless you have a reason.
2. **Retention beats virality now.** Roblox's 28-day recommendation window means a game that keeps 10-year-olds coming back for a month beats a flash-in-the-pan meme game.
3. **Expect lower spend per kid.** Roblox flagged falling per-hour spend from younger US/CA players. Design for many small, fair purchases and Premium engagement, not a few whales.
4. **Design mobile-first, low-spec.** Mobile is 84% of players worldwide, hardware is getting pricier, and growth markets (India, MEA, LatAm) run cheaper phones.
5. **Localize early.** Japan and India are Roblox's fastest-growing big markets. Icons-first UI makes translation cheap.
6. **Plan around big releases.** GTA VI (Nov 19, 2026) will flood YouTube. It's a weak window for buying attention aimed at teens, though less so for under-13s.
7. **Platform risk is real.** Rec Room shut down. Roblox changed its algorithm and safety rules in 2026 and bookings dropped. Keep your game's design, art and code portable where you can, and read every quarterly letter.
