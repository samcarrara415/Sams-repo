# UGC Platforms Compared: Where Should You Build?

> **What you'll learn**
> - How Roblox, Fortnite (UEFN), Minecraft and others compare on audience, payouts, discovery and tools (as of Oct 2026).
> - Exactly how each one pays creators, and what that rewards.
> - A decision table: who should build where. Short answer for Sam: **Roblox first**, with Fortnite as a possible second platform once you're 18+ and comfortable with Unreal.

**Tags:** **[P]** platform said it · **[S]** analyst/press · **[U]** unverified. As of Oct 2026. Payout rules change often, so re-check the official page before you plan money around them.

## 2. Roblox

**Audience** [P] ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm))
- 123M daily active users, 29B hours in Q2 2026. Down from a 151.5M peak in Q3 2025 [S], but still the biggest kids' platform.
- Age split of age-checked users: 35% under 13, 38% 13–17, 27% 18+.
- Fastest growth: Japan +67%, India +64%; US/Canada +6%.

**How creators get paid** [P]
- **DevEx rate: $0.0038 per earned Robux** for Robux earned from Sept 5, 2025 (was $0.0035) ([DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-devex-%E2%80%94-creators-will-now-earn-85-more/3920159)). 10,000 Robux = $38.
- **2025 total DevEx: $1.503B**; top 1,000 creators averaged **$1.3M** each [P] ([Q4 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm)).
- Q2 2026 DevEx: $363M (+15%) = about 23% of bookings [P].
- **Creator Rewards** (since July 24, 2025) [P] ([Creator docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/creator-rewards.md)):
  - *Daily Engagement:* **5 Robux** each day an "Active Spender" (spent ≥ $9.99 on Roblox in the past 60 days) plays your game ≥10 minutes, if it's one of the first three games they open that day.
  - *Audience Expansion:* **35%** of a new or returning user's first **$100** of purchases anywhere on Roblox in their first 60 days, if they joined via your share link, direct link or a search for your game's name. Needs ID-verified account.
- From June 8, 2026, games using R15 avatars earn **37.8% (was 26.6%)** of in-game spend from age-checked 18+ US users [P] ([Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm)).
- DevEx eligibility (minimum earned Robux, age, account standing) is on the Roblox Help Center. Historically 13+ and 30,000 earned Robux [U]. **Re-check before you plan.**

**Discovery** [P] (Q2 2026 letter)
- Recommended For You now looks at **28 days** of behaviour (was 7), and uses **play-through rate** and **first-play bounce rate**.
- Games outside the top 10 drove 65% of Robux-spend growth in Q1 2026 [P]. The long tail is getting more of the pie.
- Kids (5–8) and Select (9–15) accounts only see a curated catalog (~30,000 games by Q2 2026) [P]. Being in it matters.

**Tools:** Roblox Studio + Luau; free; built-in hosting, payments, analytics, ads. Nearly half of the top 1,000 creators use Studio Assistant or MCP (AI tools) [P] (Q1 2026 letter).

**Risks:** policy changes hit fast (age checks, cross-game pass sales disabled, algorithm change) and bookings guidance for Q3 2026 is −14% to −18% [P]. Lots of safety scrutiny from regulators (e.g. Ofcom) [S].

## 3. Fortnite (UEFN / Fortnite Creative)

**Audience**
- 75M+ monthly active users; **player-made islands took 47% of all Fortnite hours in May 2026**, up from 38% a year earlier [S] ([PocketGamer.biz, June 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/unreal-engine-for-fortnite-creator-payouts-surpass-1bn/)).
- Fortnite playtime on PC/console fell 29% in 2025 while Roblox rose 52% [S] ([Newzoo via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-pc-and-console-market-in-2026)).
- Every island gets an IARC age rating (E to Teen); parents can block by rating [S] ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/consoles-pc/fortnite-will-soon-require-an-age-rating-for-player-made-content)).

**How creators get paid**
- **Engagement payouts:** 40% of eligible Fortnite net revenue (after platform fees) goes into an engagement pool, shared monthly across all islands, Epic's own included, by engagement from paying players [P] ([Epic docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite-creative/engagement-payout-in-fortnite-creative)). Sponsored Row revenue also feeds the pool (100% through end of 2026) [P] (same).
- **In-island transactions** (live since Jan 9, 2026): creators get **100% of V-Bucks value through Jan 31, 2027, then 50% from Feb 1, 2027** [P] (Fortnite news post, via [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/epic-games-rolls-out-in-island-transactions-for-fortnite-creative)). Items are capped at 5,000 V-Bucks and random items must show odds [S] ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-gluecksspiel-mechaniken,1545726/)).
- 3,000+ islands earn more from in-island sales than from engagement payouts [S] (PocketGamer.biz, June 2026).
- **$1B+ paid to UEFN creators since 2023** [S]; 2024 payouts reported at ~$352M [U] ([Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1602861/fortnite-ugc-user-engagement)).
- **You must be 18+** to receive engagement payouts; $100 minimum payout threshold [P] ([Epic docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/island-creator-and-engagement-program-in-fortnite-creative)).

**Discovery:** Discover rows (algorithmic + editorial), creator codes, sponsored row, island codes shared by YouTubers. Top islands are visible on fortnite.gg [U]. *Steal the Brainrot* (an indie Fortnite island) reportedly peaked near 1.09M concurrent players [U] ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/fortnite-steal-the-brainrot-gluecksspiel-mechaniken,1545726/)), so Roblox trends can be ported.

**Tools:** UEFN (Unreal Editor for Fortnite) + Verse. Much higher visual ceiling, steeper learning curve, PC-heavy workflow. Fortnite's art style and characters are fixed, which saves art time.

## 4. Minecraft

- **Audience:** 155M monthly active users, an all-time high (Microsoft, Oct 29, 2025 earnings call, covering Jul–Sep 2025) [P] ([Microsoft IR](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q1)). Third-party trackers claim ~212M [U]; ignore unless Microsoft confirms.
- **Marketplace (Bedrock):** creators sell skins, worlds, texture packs and add-ons for Minecoins. **$500M+ paid to creators since 2017** [U] ([CORQ, June 2026](https://corq.studio/insights/minecraft-marketplace-inside-the-platforms-500-million-creator-economy/), citing secondary stats). ~295 partner organisations [U].
- **Who can sell:** an approved **Marketplace Partner** (usually a company or team). Not "upload and earn" like Roblox [U]. Check minecraft.net's creator pages for current rules.
- **Revenue share:** often reported as ~70% to creator, unconfirmed [U] (CORQ).
- **Discovery:** curated store and featured placements. No public CCU charts. Hard to research.
- **Also:** Java servers (own monetization rules, EULA limits) and a 2026 affiliate program [U].

## 6. What each payout model rewards

| Model | Rewards | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Roblox sales + DevEx** | Things players *want to buy* | Temptation to push kids to spend. Keep it fair. |
| **Roblox Creator Rewards** | Paying players choosing you **first** each day; bringing **new/returning** players via share links | Only spenders count for Daily Engagement; your non-paying kid fans earn you nothing directly. |
| **Fortnite engagement pool** | Time from paying players; new/returning players | Your share depends on everyone else's islands too. 18+ only. |
| **Fortnite in-island sales** | Items in your island | Rate drops from 100% to 50% Feb 2027. Random items draw scrutiny. |
| **Minecraft Marketplace** | Polished, family-safe content packs | Gatekept partner program; slow. |
| **Steam** | A game people pay for upfront | Marketing is on you. Very long tail. |

## 7. Decision table: who should build where

| If you are… | Build on | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo, under 18, kids audience, mobile** | **Roblox** | Biggest under-13 audience, free tools, hosting and payments built in. Check DevEx age rules. |
| **Solo adult, kids audience (Sam)** | **Roblox first**, then optionally port a proven hit to **Fortnite** | Roblox's audience and Luau speed. Fortnite adds a second income stream with fixed art style, 18+ OK. |
| **Strong Unreal/3D artist, teen/young adult audience** | **Fortnite UEFN** | Higher visual ceiling; 47% of Fortnite hours already go to islands. |
| **Small studio making family-safe content packs** | **Minecraft Marketplace** | Huge kid brand, but you must be accepted as a partner. |
| **Want to own the IP and sell a premium game** | **Steam** (or s&box export) | Full control, but no built-in kid audience and harsh odds. |
| **Avatar/fashion items only** | Roblox UGC, Zepeto | Item creators, not game devs. |

**Rule of thumb:** pick the platform where **your target players already spend their afternoons**, then check you can legally get paid there.
