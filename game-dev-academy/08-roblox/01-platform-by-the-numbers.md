### How to read the 2025–2026 story

1. **2025 was a viral year.** A few huge hits pulled in record players. DAU in Q3 2025 was up 70% and hours up 91% YoY [P] [Q3'25][q3-25].
2. **Early 2026 cooled off for 3 reasons Roblox itself names** [P] [Q1'26][q1-26], [Q2'26][q2-26]:
   - **Mandatory age checks for chat** (Jan 2026) cut on-platform chat. Roblox guided a "mid-single-digit engagement headwind."
   - **Players moved from 2025's high-spending viral hits to newer and evergreen games** that earn less per hour.
   - **The new Recommended For You algorithm** deliberately pushes retentive games "at the expense of near term monetization". The hit was bigger than expected for younger US/Canada players.
3. **The platform is less top-heavy.** The top 10 games took **~20% of hours in Q2 2026, down from ~30%** three years earlier. Games outside the top 10 grew hours **+25%** and Robux spend **+20%+** YoY [P] [Q2'26][q2-26].

Point 3 is the good news for a small dev.

## 3. Who plays: age (new age-checked data)

From Q1 2026, Roblox stopped using self-reported age and switched to **age-checked** users (face estimation or ID). Roblox says these figures are estimates and **not comparable to older self-reported data**. Roblox's Q4 2025 supplemental materials say the age-checked data shows a **younger** player base than the self-reported data did [P] [Q4'25 supplemental][q4-25-supp], [Q1'26][q1-26].

| Snapshot (7 days ended) | % age-checked of DAU | Under 13 | 13–17 | 18+ | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jan 31, 2026 | 45% | 35% | 38% | 27% | [P] [Roblox newsroom Feb 2026][age-feb26] |
| Mar 31, 2026 | 51% | 36% | 38% | 26% | [P] [Q1'26][q1-26] |
| Jun 30, 2026 | 57% | 35% | 38% | 27% | [P] [Q2'26][q2-26] |

- **About 73% of age-checked users are under 18** (35 + 38). "7 in 10 players are kids/teens" holds up.
- **18+ is the smallest of the three groups globally (~27%).** Older claims that "18+ is now the largest group" came from self-reported data and **do not hold** under age checks.
- **18+ is the fastest-growing group in the US:** US 18+ DAU **+32%**, hours **+27%** YoY. US **18–34** DAU **+42%**. 18+ is about **one-third** of US age-checked DAU. US 18+ players **monetize 50%+ higher** than under-18s [P] [Q2'26][q2-26].
- **Caveat:** only 57% of DAU are age-checked (60% of under-18s globally, 75% in the US). Users who skip age checks may be different [P] [Q2'26][q2-26].

## 8. What Roblox is pushing next (affects your plans)

From RDC 2026 (Sep 11, 2026) and the Q2 2026 letter [P] [RDC 2026][rdc26], [Q2'26][q2-26]:

- **Discovery is age-aware** and optimizes over **28 days**. Roblox is also testing a reward for games that bring **new players** to the platform.
- **Moments** (short video feed on the home screen) is fully live in the US for some age groups. Videos from your game page will appear there.
- **Web play in Chrome** by end of 2026. **Offline solo play** by mid-2027. **Standalone app publishing** is "soon".
- **2D game support** (orthographic camera, sprite sheets) and **turn-based async notifications** (beta Oct 2026).
- **Build:** prompt-based game creation in the mobile app. ~9,000 games made in the alpha. Expect a lot more competing content.
- **Roblox Wallet:** daily payouts for eligible US 18+ independent creators later in 2026.

## 9. What this means for Sam (solo, tower defense, mobile-first)

1. **The long tail is growing.** Top-10 share fell from ~30% to ~20% of hours [P]. A small, retentive game has more room than it did in 2023.
2. **Retention is the currency.** Roblox's own algorithm now trades short-term spend for 28-day retention [P]. Tower-defense games already over-index here: in the Oct 26, 2026 snapshot, **8 TD games** sat in the "Top Revisited" chart (players who come back after a week) [P data, our count] [charts-api].
3. **Most of your players won't pay, and most aren't American.** Daily payer rate is ~1.4% platform-wide (calc). US/Canada is ~18% of players and ~61% of revenue. Use regional pricing and make the free game great.
4. **Your audience is young.** ~73% of age-checked players are under 18 [P]. Kids & Select accounts (5–15) only see games that pass Roblox's evaluation (see 08.05). If your TD game is kid-friendly, getting in matters.
5. **Expect the numbers to move.** Q3 2026 bookings are guided **down 14–18% YoY**. Platform money is softer in 2026 than in the 2025 boom. Plan budgets on your own analytics, not platform headlines.

- telling bookings apart from revenue,
- knowing the newest numbers and their dates,
- noticing the switch from self-reported to age-checked data,
- refusing to use platform averages as per-game benchmarks,
