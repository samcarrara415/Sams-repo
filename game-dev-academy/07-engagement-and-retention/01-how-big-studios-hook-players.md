# How the Big Studios Get Players Coming Back

> **What you'll learn**
> - The real playbook from Supercell, King, Epic, Nintendo, HoYoverse, Niantic, Riot, Mojang and the Roblox mega-hits.
> - The psychology underneath it, with a **kid-safe version** and a **dark-pattern version** of every tactic.
> - Short version: the games that last make players **want** to return (friends, mastery, surprises, their own creations). The tricks that make players feel they **can't** stop are the ones regulators are now fining.

Tags: **[P]** primary (the company, a regulator, or a peer-reviewed paper), **[S]** secondary (reputable press or analysts), **[U]** unverified (community data, trackers, wikis). Dates say when the number applied. Researched Oct 2026.

---

## 1. The one idea to hold onto

Big studios don't have a secret "addiction button". They have three things:

1. **A core loop that's fun in 30 seconds.** Play a round, get a result, want another.
2. **A meta loop that gives the next round a reason.** Unlocks, upgrades, collections, ranks.
3. **A live calendar that gives tomorrow a reason.** Events, seasons, new content, friends online.

Research backs the "want" version. Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski (2006) ran four studies and found that feeling **autonomy** (my choices matter), **competence** (I'm getting better) and **relatedness** (people I care about are here) each predicted enjoyment and future play [P] ([Motivation and Emotion, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8)).

So a hook that serves those three needs is good design. A hook that runs on pressure, confusion or guilt is a dark pattern. With kids it is also a legal risk (see [03-ethics-and-law-for-kids-games.md](03-ethics-and-law-for-kids-games.md)).

## 2. Studio by studio

### Supercell (Clash Royale, Brawl Stars, Clash of Clans)

| What they do | Detail and source |
|---|---|
| **Kill most games** | In Feb 2023 CEO Ilkka Paananen said Supercell had killed 30+ games and launched 5 hits [S] ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/5-lessons-from-ilkka-paananens-bafta-games-lecture)). Small "cells" of about five people own each game [S] ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/?p=130961)). |
| **Kill even after launch** | Squad Busters shipped globally but had weak retention, so it is being shut down. Paananen's lessons: big betas don't predict global behaviour, test the meta and long-term retention, and don't spend big on marketing before the vision is proven [S] ([mobilegamer.biz, Feb 10 2026](https://mobilegamer.biz/supercell-boss-laments-coasting-mobile-industry-as-clash-royale-stars-in-2025-results/)). |
| **Fix the meta, not just the content** | Clash Royale's 2025 comeback came from reworked progression, **removing** extra systems, and new content. Re-engaged players doubled and new players grew "almost 500%" in 2025 [S] (same source, Feb 2026). |
| **Make ranks forgiving** | Brawl Stars' Oct 2024 Trophy Season rework said the old system was "unnecessarily complex and not fun". It discouraged playing your favourite Brawler. The new system: 50 tiers to 1,000 trophies, gentler losses below 1,000, a monthly reset aligned with the Brawl Pass season [P] ([Supercell blog, Oct 15 2024](https://supercell.com/en/games/brawlstars/blog/news/trophy-season-rework-is-coming)). |
| **Scale** | Supercell 2025 revenue €2.65bn (down 4%), about 290m monthly active players [S] ([mobilegamer.biz, Feb 2026](https://mobilegamer.biz/supercell-boss-laments-coasting-mobile-industry-as-clash-royale-stars-in-2025-results/)). |

**Steal this:** prototype fast, judge on D1 *and* long-term retention, and delete systems that don't pull their weight. "Simpler" brought Clash Royale back.

### King (Candy Crush Saga)

- **Level design at scale.** King keeps a steady flow of new levels. Its AI Labs trained playtest bots on player data to play *like humans*, not to win. Designers use them to tune difficulty, and **designers make the final call** [S] ([mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/how-king-balances-human-and-ai-powered-design-in-candy-crush-saga/)).
- **Near-misses.** Failing a level by one or two moves feels like "almost!". It invites another try.
- **Lives.** A limited number of tries that refill over time. This creates natural session breaks and a reason to come back later. I did not verify the current life count or refill time, so check in-game.

**Kid-safe version:** use a near-miss only when the player really was close. Show them what to try next. Let lives refill through play or time, never through guilt.
**Dark version:** fake near-misses (rigging the board so the player loses by 1). Paywalls at an "almost won" moment. Popups that say "Your friends are waiting!".

### Epic Games (Fortnite)

| Mechanic | Detail and source |
|---|---|
| **Battle pass and seasons** | Every season has a pass: a free track, a paid track and a clear finish line. Since Dec 1 2024 the Fortnite Crew subscription includes the Battle Pass, LEGO Pass, Music Pass and OG Pass [P] ([Fortnite news, Nov 20 2024](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-crew-will-include-the-battle-pass-lego-pass-music-pass-and-more-starting-in-december)). V-Buck prices for the passes differ between sources, so check in-game. |
| **Live events** | Travis Scott's "Astronomical" concert drew 12.3M concurrent players, per Epic (Apr 2020) [S] ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/fortnite/concurrent-player-record)). The OG season had 44.7M players in one day (Nov 2023) [S] ([Naavik, Sep 2026](https://naavik.co/weekly-digest/tis-the-season-holiday-live-ops-in-2026/)). |
| **Item shop rotation** | Cosmetics rotate in and out. Scarcity drives return visits, and it is also where FOMO lives. |
| **Creator economy** | 40% of Item Shop and related net revenue goes into an engagement pool, paid to island publishers by engagement (started Mar 2023) [P] ([Fortnite](https://www.fortnite.com/news/introducing-the-creator-economy-2-0)). Epic paid creators $352M in 2024, and creator islands were 36.5% of Fortnite playtime [S] ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/epic-games-paid-352-million-to-fortnite-creators-in-2024), [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/fortnite-by-the-numbers-342m-paid-to-creators-in-2024-with-creators-tripling-to-70000/)). |
| **The cautionary tale** | FTC, Dec 19 2022: $275M COPPA penalty plus $245M in refunds. The FTC cited voice and text chat on by default for kids, "counterintuitive, inconsistent, and confusing button configuration" causing unwanted purchases, and accounts locked after disputed charges [P] ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/12/fortnite-video-game-maker-epic-games-pay-more-half-billion-dollars-over-ftc-allegations)). |

**Steal this:** seasons with a visible finish line, and live moments everyone shares. **Never** copy the 2017–2022 purchase flow.

### Nintendo (Animal Crossing, Pokémon)

- **Real-time clock.** Animal Crossing runs on the console clock. Shops open and close, seasons change, and bugs and fish change with the calendar. In a 2014 Iwata Asks interview, director Aya Kyogoku described wanting the game to fit the player's real life. The team treated a shared sense of seasons and time passing in sync with the real world as a core part of the series (paraphrased) [P] ([Iwata Asks, New Leaf](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/3ds/animalcrossing-newleaf/0/1)).
- **Gentle return reasons.** Something new each day (a visitor, a shop item), but **no punishment** for missing a day beyond weeds and a sad-looking villager.
- **Collection.** The museum and Pokédex give a long, calm goal you fill at your own pace.

**Steal this:** "Come back tomorrow, there will be something new" instead of "come back tomorrow or lose your streak".

### miHoYo / HoYoverse (Genshin Impact)

- **Gacha with pity.** A featured 5-star is guaranteed within 90 character-banner wishes, and losing a "50/50" guarantees the featured unit next time [S] ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/pity-soft-pity-50-50-genshin-impact-explained)). "Soft pity" (odds rising after ~74 wishes) is a community observation, not an official term [U].
- **Resin (energy cap).** Original Resin regenerates 1 point per 8 minutes, and the cap was raised from 160 to 200 in v4.7 (June 2024) [S] ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/genshin-impact-will-increase-its-resin-cap-in-version-4-7/)). The cap paces progress and gives a "check back" rhythm.
- **The FTC case.** Jan 17 2025: $20M penalty. The FTC alleged that HoYoverse deceived players about 5-star odds, used confusing multi-step currencies, marketed to kids, and broke COPPA. The proposed order bans loot box sales to under-16s without parental consent and requires disclosed odds and exchange rates [P] ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/genshin-impact-game-developer-will-be-banned-selling-lootboxes-teens-under-16-without-parental)).

**For Sam's audience:** paid gacha is out for kids (and restricted on Roblox in several countries). Pity is still a good idea for **free** random drops: it guarantees that bad luck ends.

### Niantic (Pokémon GO)

- **Community Day.** Since Jan 2018: once a month, one featured Pokémon spawns everywhere for a few hours, with an exclusive move [P] ([Pokémon GO](https://pokemongo.com/post/communityday?hl=en)). It's an **appointment** players plan around with friends.
- **Owned events beat holidays.** GO Fest 2025 reached 6.7× a normal week's revenue, compared with 1.1× for its best holiday week [S] ([Naavik, Sep 2026](https://naavik.co/weekly-digest/tis-the-season-holiday-live-ops-in-2026/)).

**Steal this:** a short, predictable, monthly event that is fun **with friends**.

### Riot Games (League of Legends, Valorant)

- **Ranked seasons:** a ladder that resets on a schedule. That gives everyone a fresh climb (competence).
- **Social systems:** parties, clubs and honor-style recognition (relatedness).
- **Monetisation is mostly cosmetic.** I did not re-verify Riot's current numbers or policies in this pass, so this is a qualitative summary only.

### Mojang (Minecraft)

- **The creativity loop:** gather, build, show a friend, get an idea, gather more. Players set their own goals (autonomy).
- It needs no timers or streaks to bring players back, because **the player's own creation** is the reason.
- I didn't verify current player counts in this pass.

### Roblox mega-hits

| Game | What hooks players | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Adopt Me!** | Raise pets, hatch eggs, **trade** with friends. The economy is social. It hit 1.9M concurrent users in Apr 2021, a Roblox record at the time. | [S] [PocketGamer.biz 2021](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/76516/roblox-adopt-me-devs-launch-uplift-games/) |
| **Grow a Garden** | Plants grow while you're offline, seed stock rotates, and weekly exclusive items need you online. Peaks: 21.3M CCU on Jun 21 2025 and 22.3M on Aug 23 2025. | [S] [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/roblox-grow-a-garden-shatters-all-time-player-record-for-second-week-in-a-row/); [U] [Wikipedia citing RoMonitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden) |
| **The flip side** | Grow a Garden fell out of Roblox's top 10 by Dec 2025. Steal a Brainrot was down ~30% in monthly visits from its Aug peak. Brookhaven RP and Blox Fruits were in the top 10 all 12 months. | [S] [Newzoo via GameDev Reports, Apr 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-top-roblox-games-in-2025) |

**Lesson:** restock FOMO makes spikes. Social play and long-term goals make games that stay.

## 3. The psychology, tactic by tactic

Each row lists how the tactic works, who uses it, the version we build, and the version we never build.

| Tactic | How it works | Who uses it | ✅ Kid-safe version | ❌ Dark-pattern version |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Core loop** | Short action → feedback → reward → repeat | Everyone | A 30–90s round with clear wins | Rounds padded with ads or waits |
| **Meta loop** | Rewards feed long goals (unlocks, upgrades) | Supercell, Fortnite | Upgrades earned through play, shown on a map | Upgrades only realistic with money (pay-to-win) |
| **Compulsion loop vs. motivation** | Rewards on a schedule *can* drive play without enjoyment. SDT says lasting play comes from autonomy, competence and relatedness [P] ([Ryan et al. 2006](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8)) | Whole industry | Design for the three needs first | Optimise "time in app" whether or not kids enjoy it |
| **Variable rewards** | Unpredictable rewards are exciting (the slot-machine effect) | Gacha, loot drops | Free random drops with **shown odds** and pity. Cosmetic only | Paid random boxes, hidden odds, nested currencies. Linked to problem gambling in a correlational study of 7,422 gamers [P] ([Zendle & Cairns 2018](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/139116/1/journal.pone.0206767.pdf)) |
| **Collection and completion** | An incomplete set nags at us | Pokémon, Animal Crossing, Adopt Me | Index or museum filled by play. Trading among friends | Sets that are only completable with paid boxes |
| **Loss aversion** | Losing feels worse than winning feels good | Streaks, decaying resources | Small, recoverable setbacks | "Your pet will die / crops will rot unless you pay" |
| **Social obligation** | We don't want to let friends down | Clans, co-op | Optional co-op bonuses. Gifts that never expire | "Your team needs you NOW" pushes. Shaming absent friends |
| **Appointment mechanics** | A thing happens at a set time | Community Day, Animal Crossing | Predictable weekly or monthly events, posted ahead | Random flash events that punish kids at school or asleep |
| **Streaks** | Don't break the chain | Many apps | Streak **freezes**, catch-up days, rewards that don't reset to zero. The EU minors guidelines push for streaks **off by default** for minors [S] ([IRIS Merlin](https://merlin.obs.coe.int/article/10383)) | Lose everything after one missed day |
| **FOMO / scarcity** | "Only now!" makes things feel valuable | Item shops, restocks | Real seasonal items that **return** later. Honest timers | Fake countdowns, fake "only 3 left!", fake discounts. Groups asked the FTC in May 2026 to investigate Roblox games' "scarcity marketing" [S] ([News Tribune](https://www.newstribune.com/news/2026/may/22/child-safety-groups-want-ftc-to-investigate-roblox/)) |
| **Goal gradient** | People speed up near a goal (coffee-card study, Kivetz, Urminsky & Zheng 2006) | Battle passes | Progress bars with achievable steps | Last tier made deliberately slow to push a "skip tiers" purchase |
| **Endowed progress** | A head start raises completion. In a car-wash study, 34% finished a pre-stamped card vs 19% with a blank card [P] ([Nunes & Drèze 2006](https://msbfile03.usc.edu/digitalmeasures/jnunes/intellcont/Endowed%20Progress%20Effect-1.pdf)) | Onboarding, passes | Start a new player at "2 of 10" on the first quest | Fake "you're 90% there, pay to finish" |
| **Sunk cost** | "I've put so much in, I can't stop" | Long grinds, gacha | Let players take breaks without losing what they built | Make leaving feel like losing everything |
| **Juice / game feel** | Screen shake, particles, sound, squash and stretch make actions feel good ("Juice It or Lose It", Jonasson & Purho, 2012; Swink, *Game Feel*, 2009) [S] ([overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_feel)) | Everyone good | Juice the **gameplay** (hits, upgrades, wins) | Juice only the **shop** (confetti on purchase, sad sounds on "no") |

Hook models such as Nir Eyal's *Hooked* (trigger → action → variable reward → investment) describe the same loop. Use them to understand the loop, not as a checklist for kids.

**Why this matters for kids:** a 2022 study of 160 apps used by 3–5-year-olds found manipulative design in about 80% of them (fake time pressure, lures, parasocial pressure). Children from lower-income families saw more of it [P] ([Radesky et al., JAMA Netw Open 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9206186/)).

## 4. Put it together: a tower-defense loop

```
CORE (60–120s)   place towers → wave → juicy kills → "Wave cleared!" → coins
META (days)      coins → upgrade tower tree → unlock new map → collection index
SOCIAL           co-op maps with friends, gifting, show off a tower skin
LIVE (weeks)     weekly challenge map, monthly season pass (free track first), seasonal events
```

Checks before you ship any hook:
1. Would a kid **choose** this if no timer or popup were pushing?
2. Can a free player finish the season?
3. Would you be fine with a parent reading the screen over the kid's shoulder?
