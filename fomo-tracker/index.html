How the scores work

FOMO score (0 to 100)

A momentum score: the sum of four parts, each capped.

  • Price momentum, up to 45. The 1-hour change counts most (+150% or more earns 28 points), then the 5-minute change (+50% earns 9) and the 6-hour change (+300% earns 8). Price drops subtract points.
  • Buyers, up to 30. Up to 18 for unique buying wallets in the last 24 hours, on a log scale (2,000 or more earns all 18), plus up to 12 when last-hour buyers run above the pool's average hourly rate (4 times the average earns all 12). For pools younger than 24 hours the average uses the pool's real age.
  • Buy pressure, up to 15. How far the share of buys among last-hour trades is above 50% (all buys earns 15). Needs at least 5 trades in the hour, otherwise 0.
  • Turnover, up to 10. 24-hour volume divided by market cap, or by liquidity if that is larger. Volume of 2 times that value or more earns all 10.

Risk score (0 to 100)

Starts at 100 and loses points for each warning below. Pools under one hour old are capped at 60, so they can never show as lower risk.

Bands: 70 and above is lower risk, 45 to 69 is caution, below 45 is high risk.

Data notes

  • Each refresh loads page 1 of GeckoTerminal's new pools and trending pools for every selected chain (up to 20 + 20 pools per chain).
  • Market cap falls back to FDV when GeckoTerminal doesn't report it, which is common for new tokens.
  • "Buyers" are unique wallets that bought through the pool, as reported by GeckoTerminal. They are not on-chain holder counts.
  • When a token has several pools, the one with the deepest liquidity is shown. Pools where the base token is SOL, ETH, BNB or a stablecoin are skipped, because their price and FDV describe that asset rather than the new token.
  • "Boosted" means the token currently has a paid DexScreener boost. That is marketing spend, not a quality signal.
